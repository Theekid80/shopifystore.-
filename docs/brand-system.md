# WEDRA brand system

## 1. What WEDRA is

**WEDRA finds the products worth discovering.**

WEDRA is a curation brand for wellness and everyday living. It searches,
filters and presents a small number of useful, well-positioned products that
make everyday life better, easier, calmer or more enjoyable. The customer
should feel that the searching has already been done for them.

| WEDRA is | WEDRA is not |
| --- | --- |
| The finder and curator | The manufacturer of what it features |
| A destination for considered discoveries | A one-product blender brand |
| Editorial, calm, credible | A "viral products" or discount store |
| Honest about what a product does | A supplement, medical or treatment company |
| Premium in presentation | Fake luxury |

The customer should think *"WEDRA finds interesting wellness products for
me"*, never *"WEDRA makes blenders"*. When the next product arrives, they
should think *"WEDRA found another one."*

## 2. Brand vs product

Keep two layers of message apart.

| Layer | Scope | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand** | Every page, every channel, every product | WEDRA. Products worth discovering. |
| **Discovery label** | Frames each product as a find | WEDRA Discovery #01 |
| **Product** | One product's campaign only | Your smoothie. Your shake. Your routine. Anywhere. |

Product lines can be specific about portability, routines and use cases.
They must never become the brand's identity, the homepage headline or the
site's SEO title on their own.

## 3. Brand language

Use as direction, not as slogans stamped on every section. Pick the line that
fits the visual.

- Products worth discovering. *(primary)*
- Discover better.
- Wellness worth discovering.
- Curated for better everyday living.
- Better finds for everyday life.
- Thoughtfully found.
- We search. We filter. You discover.
- Less searching. Better finds.
- One discovery today. More to come.
- We found something worth showing you. *(video opener)*

## 4. Voice

**Confident, concise, modern, intelligent, tasteful, editorial, approachable,
curious, useful.** Write like someone who has already filtered the noise.

Do:
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Say what the product is and where it fits.
- Use the real numbers (350 ml, 50 W) instead of adjectives.

Don't:
- "Viral", "TikTok made me buy it", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "must-have".
- "Buy now before it's gone", countdowns, "only 3 left", "10,000 sold".
- Exclamation marks in brand copy; emoji beyond an occasional one on social.
- Wellness clichés ("detox", "cleanse", "boost your immunity", "burn fat").
- Medical, nutritional, weight-loss or body-transformation claims.
- Corporate jargon ("solutions", "leverage", "best-in-class").

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| The #1 viral blender everyone needs!!! | A compact blender for your everyday routine. |
| Blends anything in seconds | A 50 W motor with dual stainless-steel blades. |
| Boost your health on the go | Make a smoothie or shake wherever your day goes. |
| Designed by WEDRA | Found by WEDRA. |

## 5. Trust rules (non-negotiable)

- No fake reviews, testimonials, star ratings, customer counts, press
  mentions, awards or certifications.
- No fake scarcity, countdown timers or invented discounts.
- No claim that WEDRA designed, manufactured or "personally tested" a product
  unless that has actually happened.
- No supplier names, supplier pricing or sourcing details anywhere public.
- Specifications only from the verified product source (see the product's
  fact sheet in `docs/`).
- Never put a WEDRA logo on a product that doesn't carry one.

## 6. Visual identity

**Premium editorial wellness**: clean, modern, minimal, natural, warm,
understated. The product is always the visual focus.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm white | `#F7F5F0` | Page background, hero |
| Stone | `#EDEAE3` | Alternating sections, image grounds |
| Ink | `#111111` | Text, primary buttons |
| Soft grey | `#67645E` | Secondary text |
| Charcoal | `#161615` | Dark statement sections, end frames |
| Muted sage | `#6F7A63` | Rare accent (links, small details) |
| Lines | `#DCD8D0` | Hairline rules |

- **Type**: Cormorant Garamond (headlines) + Manrope (text, labels). Labels in
  small caps-style uppercase with wide tracking.
- **Space**: generous. Let one idea own a section.
- **Photography**: natural light, real materials, realistic hands, warm
  neutrals, subtle greens from real ingredients or plants. No neon, no loud
  gradients, no badges, no Amazon-style collages.

## 7. Reusable components

The site is built from product-agnostic parts (see the README for the
section names): discovery card, featured discovery, discovery highlights,
product detail, everyday use (editorial story), lifestyle image, video block,
brand philosophy, FAQ, discovery carousel, email sign-up. Adding a product
means filling in Shopify fields, not editing code.

## 8. Discovery numbering

Each featured product gets a sequential label: **WEDRA Discovery #01**,
**#02**, and so on. Use it on the homepage eyebrow, the end frame of
"WEDRA Discovery" videos and social posts. Numbers are never reused.

## 9. Growth strategy

1. One product, done well.
2. Build demand until it reaches **$10,000 in sales**.
3. Only then add Discovery #02, chosen for the same wellness and
   everyday-living audience.
4. Never turn the store into a random catalogue. Categories (Wellness,
   Everyday, New discoveries) appear only when they hold real products.
