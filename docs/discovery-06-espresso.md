## Verified facts

- 60 ml capacity · self-heating
- 3-in-1: large capsules, small capsules or ground coffee
- USB Type-C charging, approx. 3–4 hours · 2500 mAh battery
- 72 W · 12 V rated voltage
- Approx. 83.5 × 245.5 × 83.5 mm
- Comes apart for rinsing
- Package: machine, colour box, USB cable, manual
