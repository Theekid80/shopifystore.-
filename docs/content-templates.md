# WEDRA content templates

One visual identity across every channel: warm whites, charcoal, Cormorant
Garamond headlines, Manrope labels, real product photos, no prices on ads,
no hype. Replace `{…}` per discovery. Keep captions short.

## Instagram / Reels

**1. Product discovery (carousel or Reel)**
- Slide/shot 1: "We found something worth showing you."
- Slide 2: product on warm white · "WEDRA Discovery #{NN}"
- Slide 3–4: lifestyle photos, one verified fact each
- Slide 5: "WEDRA — Products worth discovering."
- Caption: `{Product name}. {One-line reason it was selected.} Found by WEDRA. Link in bio.`

**2. Lifestyle demonstration (Reel, 9:16, 8–12 s)**
- Morning counter → desk → bag. On-screen text: `{Product line}`.
- Caption: `{Use case 1}. {Use case 2}. {Use case 3}. #WEDRADiscovery`

**3. Problem → solution (Reel)**
- Text 1: `{Everyday friction, stated plainly}`
- Text 2: `{What the product does, in verified terms}`
- End: WEDRA

**4. Close-up (carousel)**
- One detail per slide (lid, body, blades through the jar, port), each with
  one spec: `350 ml`, `50 W`, `304 stainless steel`, `700 mAh rechargeable`.

**5. Routine (Reel)**
- Calm morning sequence, no voice-over required. Text: `Your routine. Anywhere.`

**6. Educational explainer (carousel)**
- "What we look for" → "Why this one made it" → "What it is (specs)" →
  "What it isn't (we only claim what's verified)".

## TikTok

**Discovery hook (0–2 s):** "We found something worth showing you." or
"Found: {product type} that fits in a gym bag."

**"Found this" format:** hand places the product on a counter → text
`WEDRA Discovery #{NN}` → quick use → end frame.

**Demonstration:** single take of a simple, realistic use, with verified
facts as on-screen captions. No speed or runtime claims.

**Lifestyle use case:** one environment per video (desk, gym, day out).

**Short product story:** "We looked at a lot of {category}. This one made
it because {verified reason}."

Avoid on TikTok too: "viral", "TikTok made me buy it", "run don't walk",
countdowns, fake reactions, fake comments.

## Hashtags (use 2–4)

`#WEDRA` `#WEDRADiscovery` `#productsworthdiscovering` plus one or two
category tags (`#smoothie`, `#morningroutine`, `#deskroutine`). No
`#viral`, `#tiktokmademebuyit`.
