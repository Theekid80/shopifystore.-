## Read
- Shipping is 40–65% of the landed cost of every item. Margins are thinner than they look from product cost alone.
- Bundles are WORSE than singles under separate-parcel shipping (shipping is paid per item). They only work if Zendrop's combined-parcel shipping is meaningfully cheaper — the key number to get.
- Claims: don't use "MagSafe" (Apple trademark) or "Qi-certified" in copy until verified; say "magnetic".
